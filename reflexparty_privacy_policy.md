# Reflex Party Privacy Policy

[← Back to App Launchpad]({{ '/' | relative_url }})

_Last updated: 2026-11-03_

This Privacy Policy explains how mgobill (“we,” “us”) handles information in Reflex Party and related support services. Reflex Party is a reaction and observation game for people sharing one device, with optional solo practice. Gameplay works offline and does not require a Reflex Party account.

**Release status:** Reflex Party is not yet available on the App Store or Google Play. This policy describes the app’s local features and its planned advertising and in-app purchase features for release. Advertising and purchase processing apply when those services are enabled and used.

## 1. Information we handle

### Local settings and gameplay

The app saves settings on your device, including sound and haptic preferences, custom game selections, player count, round count, and a completed-session counter used to manage ad frequency. Scores and reaction times are calculated on the device for gameplay. We do not operate an online gameplay account, cloud leaderboard, or gameplay synchronization service.

Reflex Party does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location for gameplay. Approximate location may be inferred from network information by advertising services as described below.

### Advertising and consent

The free version will include interstitial ads through **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)**. We use Google’s **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

When advertising services are enabled, Google and participating ad providers may process:

- IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
- Device or advertising identifiers, where available and permitted by device settings and consent.
- Device, operating system, and app information.
- App/ad interactions, such as launches, taps, impressions, and video views.
- Diagnostic and performance information and fraud-prevention signals.
- Consent status and privacy choices.

This information supports ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention, and ad personalization where permitted. Declining personalized advertising may still allow contextual or limited ads and associated processing. Full Game removes interstitial ads; it does not delete information already held by advertising providers.

Learn more in [Google’s explanation of data use by partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and [Google’s advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by **Apple’s App Store** or **Google Play**. We do not receive your full payment card details. The planned Full Game upgrade is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks three/four-player play and removes interstitial ads.

To verify purchases, restore access, and check refunds or revocations, the app sends purchase evidence to our purchase-verification service when configured. This includes the app identifier, platform, product identifier, transaction/purchase identifier, verification source, and store-issued purchase token or signed transaction data. The service checks that evidence with the relevant store. Network requests also expose an IP address and may generate operational or security logs.

Verified purchase evidence is cached in secure storage on the device to retain access offline. The app may recheck it with the verification service when online. Apple and Google maintain their own payment and transaction records under their policies.

### Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any device information, screenshots, or transaction details you choose to provide. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your issue.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to provide gameplay and save preferences, manage ad frequency and consent, verify and restore purchases, prevent fraud, troubleshoot problems, answer support requests, and meet legal obligations. The app does not include a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service; advertising SDKs may collect their own interaction and diagnostic data.

## 3. Sharing and third-party services

Information may be processed by:

- **Google AdMob and participating ad providers** for advertising and related measurement, diagnostics, consent, and fraud prevention.
- **Apple and Google Play** for payment processing, purchase verification, restoration, refunds, and transaction records.
- **Hosting and email providers** as needed to operate purchase verification and respond to support requests.

We may also disclose information when legally required or to protect users, prevent fraud, or secure our services. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising-related disclosures may qualify as “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under some privacy laws; applicable consent and opt-out choices are available through advertising privacy controls where required.

Third-party policies include [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Ad destinations you open have their own privacy practices.

## 4. Your choices and requests

- Open **Settings → Ad privacy choices** when that option is displayed to review or change advertising choices. Its availability depends on the consent service’s requirements for your region.
- Use your device’s advertising identifier and privacy controls where available. Device controls do not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing.
- Change sound, haptics, and custom game settings in the app.
- Remove ordinary local app data using your device’s app-data controls where available, or uninstall the app. Secure purchase storage and device backups may persist depending on the platform; uninstalling does not cancel a purchase or erase store records.
- Email [moocbear@gmail.com](mailto:moocbear@gmail.com) to request access, correction, deletion, or help exercising applicable privacy rights. There is no Reflex Party account to delete. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or a complaint to your local data protection authority. Requests about information held independently by Apple, Google, or another ad provider may need to be directed to that provider. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed.

## 5. Retention and security

Local settings remain until replaced or removed through the device’s controls. Verified purchase evidence is retained locally to support access and restoration. Support correspondence, purchase-verification records, and security logs are retained only as needed to provide support, validate ownership, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Some records may remain in backups or legally required transaction records. Third-party providers apply their own retention policies.

Purchase verification uses HTTPS and local purchase evidence uses secure device storage. We take reasonable safeguards, but no device, transmission, or storage system is completely secure.

## 6. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or the applicable minimum age in their region, without any consent required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app or support services, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Parents and guardians can manage purchases through their device’s parental controls.

## 7. International processing

Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Privacy protections may differ from those in your country. Where required, transfers are subject to safeguards under applicable law.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features and services change. Updates will appear here with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for material changes.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email [moocbear@gmail.com](mailto:moocbear@gmail.com) with “Reflex Party privacy” in the subject line.
